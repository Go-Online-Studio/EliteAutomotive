How We Work

The Elite Process

A meticulous, multi-stage approach ensuring every vehicle receives the highest standard of care.

01

Consultation & Prep

We begin with a thorough inspection to understand your needs, followed by a multi-stage decontamination wash and clay bar treatment.

02

Correction & Protection

Our skilled technicians perform paint correction to remove swirls and scratches, restoring depth. We then apply your chosen protection.

03

Final Finish & Delivery

We address every detail, including interior spa treatment, wheels, and glass. A final quality control check ensures your vehicle is perfect.
